# 0013: Weekly planning

**Status:** Accepted (2026-10-11). The specific rules were settled in the planning-rules interview (S19, 2026-11-04) and are listed under Rules.

## Context

Planning could be weekly, nightly, or a mix. Pickup/delivery grocery
ordering favors one planned order per week.

## Decision

Plan a configurable number of dinners once a week. The shopping list is
generated from the plan plus staples. The daily view shows today's dinner
with one-tap made/skip.

## Consequences

- The weekly session is the one regular habit larder asks for, so it must be
  short and start from a pre-filled suggested plan.
- What happens to skipped meals is decided by the planning rules.

## Rules

Settled by the owner on 2026-11-04 (slice S19).

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Planning session | Saturday evening, once a week |
| Dinners | 6 planned dinners per week and 1 open night. The open night is chosen when planning |
| Repeat gap | A meal is not suggested again within 10 days of the last time it was made or planned |
| Favorites | Favorites rank higher in suggestions |
| Skipped meal | Rolls to the next open night |
| Effort balance | At most one project meal per week, and only on a weekend |
| Who plans | Everyone can plan; any paired person can edit the plan |
| Breakfast and lunch | Not planned. Their ingredients are staples |
