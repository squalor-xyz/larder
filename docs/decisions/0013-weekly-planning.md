# 0013: Weekly planning

**Status:** Accepted (2026-10-11). The specific rules are settled in the planning-rules interview slice.

## Context

Planning could be weekly, nightly, or a mix. Pickup/delivery grocery
ordering favors one planned order per week.

## Decision

Plan a configurable number of dinners once a week. The shopping list is
generated from the plan plus staples. The daily view shows today's dinner
with one-tap made/skip.

## Consequences

- The weekly session is the one regular habit larder asks for, so it must be
  short and start from a pre-filled suggested plan.
- What happens to skipped meals is decided by the planning rules.
